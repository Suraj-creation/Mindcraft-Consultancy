## 2. Objective
Convince a strategy/leadership visitor that MindCraft can turn intelligence into a decision, a strategy and an executable plan — grounded in Life Sciences and cross-industry practice — and let them reach the exact sub-capability they need without reading a wall of text.

## 3. Primary visitor question
"Can they help us decide — set strategy, restructure for growth, and execute change with confidence?"

## 4. Primary audience
- Strategy leaders / heads of corporate development (primary)
- CEOs / Managing Directors evaluating a strategy partner
- Regulatory & market-access leaders (policy/regulatory advisory sub-group)
- Transformation / operations leaders (process, org design)
Secondary: government/regulator and development-sector clients (feasibility, proposals, policy).

## 5. What visitor should understand
1. Business Consulting is strategic and management advisory that helps organizations decide, restructure and execute — not generic frameworks.
2. It spans five areas: corporate & business strategy; digital & AI transformation strategy; organizational design & change; process & systems design; policy, regulatory & project advisory.
3. Outputs are applied and decision-ready (strategy docs, roadmaps, SOPs, org blueprints) — built for implementation.
4. It is one node in a connected engine: research feeds it; AI solutions and training operationalize its plans.
5. Life Sciences depth is native here (pharma/device/dx portfolio and GTM, regulatory landscape mapping).

## 8. Capability groups detail (accordion content — every source sub-bullet, cleaned)

**Group 01 — Corporate & Business Strategy** (id `corporate-strategy`)
Intro: *Set direction, plan growth, and win in new markets.*
- Corporate strategy formulation, business planning and growth-strategy development.
- Market entry and expansion strategy for new African, European or US markets.
- Portfolio strategy, product prioritization and go-to-market planning for pharmaceutical, medical device and diagnostics companies.
- Competitive positioning and brand-strategy support.

**Group 02 — Digital & AI Transformation Strategy** (id `digital-ai-strategy`)
Intro: *Diagnose readiness and build a realistic path to AI.*
- Digital maturity assessments and AI-readiness diagnostics for organizations and functions.
- Digital transformation roadmaps aligned to business strategy and budget realities.
- Technology and vendor-selection advisory for AI, CRM, ERP and data platforms.

**Group 03 — Organizational Design & Change Management** (id `org-design-change`)
Intro: *Structure the organization and carry people through change.*
- Organizational structure design, role clarity and operating-model redesign.
- Change management planning and execution support for mergers, restructuring and technology rollouts.
- Culture and change-readiness diagnostics, communication planning and stakeholder engagement.

**Group 04 — Process Improvement & Systems Design** (id `process-systems`)
Intro: *Redesign how work flows, for scale and compliance.*
- End-to-end business process mapping, redesign and optimization across commercial, operations, supply chain and quality.
- Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) design and documentation.
- Systems and workflow design to support scale, compliance and efficiency.

**Group 05 — Policy, Regulatory & Project Advisory** (id `policy-regulatory`)
Intro: *Navigate regulation and build fundable projects.*
- Policy analysis and advisory on technology adoption, healthcare and industry regulation across African markets.
- Regulatory landscape mapping and compliance strategy for pharmaceutical, medical device and chemical clients.
- Project design, feasibility studies and proposal/grant-development support for corporate and development-sector clients.
