## 8. Capability groups detail (accordion content — every source sub-bullet, cleaned)

**Group 01 — AI Strategy & Solution Architecture** (id `ai-strategy-architecture`)
Intro: *Decide what to build, and get the foundations right.*
- Enterprise AI strategy and use-case prioritization aligned to business value and data readiness.
- AI solution architecture, vendor and technology-stack selection.
- Data strategy, governance and infrastructure advisory to prepare organizations for AI deployment.

**Group 02 — Custom AI & Analytics Solutions** (id `custom-ai-analytics`)
Intro: *Build the models, platforms and dashboards you need.*
- Custom AI/ML solution design and development for commercial, operational and clinical use cases.
- AI-powered market-intelligence and competitive-monitoring platforms for Life Sciences clients.
- Business-intelligence dashboards and predictive analytics for sales, finance and operations teams.

**Group 03 — Conversational AI & Engagement Tools** (id `conversational-ai`)
Intro: *Put AI in front of patients, customers and field teams.*
- AI chatbots and virtual assistants for patient engagement, customer service and field-force support.
- AI-powered call-center and helpdesk augmentation for banking, healthcare and consumer-facing organizations.

**Group 04 — Process Automation** (id `process-automation`)
Intro: *Automate the repetitive work and connect the systems.*
- Robotic Process Automation (RPA) combined with AI for document processing, claims, invoicing and compliance workflows.
- AI integration with existing CRM, ERP and enterprise systems to embed intelligence into daily operations.

**Group 05 — Life Sciences–Specific AI Solutions** (id `life-sciences-ai`) — *cross-links to Life Sciences*
Intro: *AI purpose-built for pharma and medical device work.*
- AI-assisted pharmacovigilance and adverse-event monitoring for pharmaceutical and medical device clients.
- Regulatory-intelligence automation — tracking guideline changes, submissions and compliance deadlines across African and international regulators.
- AI-powered Key Opinion Leader (KOL) identification and market-access analytics platforms.
- Cross-link: **See these in context on** `/industries/life-sciences`.
