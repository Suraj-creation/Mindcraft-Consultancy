## 8. Capability groups detail (accordion content — every source sub-bullet, cleaned)

**Group 01 — Primary Market Research** (id `primary-research`)
Intro: *Go to the field: physicians, patients, distributors and buyers.*
- Quantitative and qualitative surveys with physicians, pharmacists, patients, distributors, retailers and institutional buyers.
- Key Opinion Leader (KOL) identification, mapping and advisory-board research for pharmaceutical and medical device clients.
- Customer, patient and consumer insight studies — satisfaction, journey and unmet-needs research.
- Distributor, channel-partner and tender/procurement intelligence gathering.

**Group 02 — Secondary & Desk Research** (id `secondary-desk`)
Intro: *Size the market, map competitors, scan the rules.*
- Market sizing, forecasting and opportunity assessment across therapeutic areas, product categories and countries.
- Competitive intelligence — pipeline tracking, pricing analysis, market-share estimation and SWOT benchmarking.
- Regulatory, policy and reimbursement landscape scans across African and international markets.
- Landscape and sector scans, including AI-adoption studies and emerging-technology assessments.

**Group 03 — Program & Impact Research** (id `program-impact`)
Intro: *Measure what programs achieve — for donors and development clients.*
- Baseline, midline and endline surveys for corporate, development-sector and donor-funded programs.
- Monitoring, Evaluation and Learning (MEL) frameworks and impact-assessment studies.
- Needs assessments and skills-gap analyses to inform capacity-building and training investments.

**Group 04 — Data, Analytics & Knowledge Products** (id `data-analytics`)
Intro: *Turn data into clean analysis and shareable knowledge.*
- Data cleaning, statistical analysis and dashboard-ready reporting.
- Custom research reports, whitepapers, toolkits and case studies for internal or public use.
- AI-assisted literature and data synthesis to accelerate turnaround without compromising rigor.
