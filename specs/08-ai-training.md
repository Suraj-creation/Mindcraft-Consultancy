## 8. Capability groups detail (accordion content — every source sub-bullet, cleaned)

**Group 01 — AI Foundations & Executive Briefings** (id `foundations-briefings`)
Intro: *Give leaders and staff a confident, shared starting point.*
- AI fundamentals and awareness training for executives, boards and staff onboarding.
- AI-readiness assessments to diagnose organizational capability and identify priority use cases.
- Sector-specific AI briefings for Life Sciences, healthcare, chemicals, automotive and financial-services leadership teams.

**Group 02 — Applied, Function-Specific AI Training** (id `applied-function`)
Intro: *Train each function on the AI it uses for real work.*
- AI for commercial and sales teams — territory planning, lead scoring and CRM-integrated insights.
- AI for market access, regulatory affairs and pharmacovigilance functions in pharmaceutical and medical device organizations.
- AI for HR, finance, communications, and monitoring & evaluation (M&E) functions.
- Prompt engineering and generative-AI tools training (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity and other leading platforms).

**Group 03 — Responsible & Governed AI Adoption** (id `responsible-ai`)
Intro: *Make AI use safe, ethical and compliant by design.*
- AI ethics, governance and responsible-use workshops, including data-privacy and confidentiality safeguards.
- Policy and guideline development for safe AI use within regulated industries such as pharmaceuticals and banking.

**Group 04 — Capability Multiplication** (id `capability-multiplication`)
Intro: *Build champions who sustain capability after we leave.*
- Train-the-trainer programs to build internal AI champions and sustain capability beyond the engagement.
- Custom curriculum design for organization-specific AI and digital-skills programs.
- Mentorship and coaching program design to reinforce classroom learning with on-the-job application.
