## **1. Company Overview** 

MindCraft Consultancy Services (“MindCraft”) is a Nairobi, Kenya-headquartered consultancy providing integrated Business Consulting, Market Research, AI Training and AI Enterprise Solutions to organizations across diversified industry verticals. Established in 2026, MindCraft was founded to close a critical gap in the market — the absence of a single, trusted partner that combines deep sector expertise with practical, applied artificial intelligence capability. 

While MindCraft serves clients across Pharmaceuticals, Medical Devices, Medical Diagnostics, Medical Equipment, Surgical Consumables, Hospitals, Chemicals, Agrochemicals, Automotive, and Banking & Finance, the firm's core expertise and heritage lie in the Life Sciences segment. This specialization allows MindCraft to bring regulatory fluency, clinical and commercial context, and industry-specific benchmarks into every engagement — whether the client is a global pharmaceutical major, a regional hospital group, or a fast-growing agrochemical distributor. 

MindCraft operates from its Nairobi headquarters with service delivery across Africa, Europe and the United States. The firm maintains a significant and deliberate focus on the African continent, where it combines on-the-ground market knowledge with international consulting standards to help clients navigate complex, fast-evolving and often under-researched markets.
