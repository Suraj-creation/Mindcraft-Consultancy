# MindCraft — Design Context

## Users
Senior decision-makers evaluating a consultancy: CEOs/MDs, strategy & commercial leaders, Life Sciences and regulatory/market-access executives, CIOs/CTOs, HR/L&D, government, NGOs, academia. Context: daytime desk/mobile evaluation, skimming first, then investigating depth. Job: quickly understand what MindCraft is, whether it understands their industry + the African context, and how to engage.

## Brand Personality
Voice: an intelligent senior consultant — precise, confident, plain-spoken; African-rooted, globally fluent. Three words: **rigorous · grounded · quietly-confident**. Emotional goal: credibility and calm authority, not hype. A 2026 firm — credibility from specialization + clarity + method, never fabricated proof.

## Aesthetic Direction
"Editorial intelligence report / field dossier." Light, warm-paper theme with deliberate dark ink bands for emphasis. Typographic hierarchy and whitespace carry the design; hairlines and structure over cards; restrained line-based diagrams and a line map of Africa. Anti-references: AI-startup slop — purple/cyan/neon gradients, glassmorphism, glowing orbs, abstract "AI brains," particle fields, endless rounded cards, fake dashboards/metrics.

### Type (non-reflex, per impeccable font procedure — supersedes specs/19 Fraunces/Inter/IBM Plex Mono for the prototype)
- Display: **Bricolage Grotesque**
- Body/reading: **Literata**
- Mono (labels, coordinates, indices; sparing): **Spline Sans Mono**

### Color (OKLCH, neutrals tinted toward accent hue)
- Paper `oklch(0.972 0.008 70)`, ink `oklch(0.22 0.014 55)`, accent terracotta `oklch(0.56 0.13 45)`, support ochre + muted slate-green for data. No pure black/white; no AI palette.

## Design Principles
1. Clarity > density; hierarchy > decoration; meaning > effects.
2. Progressive disclosure everywhere — show the essential, let the visitor choose depth.
3. Life Sciences reads visibly deeper than other industries; Africa is a strategic position, not an address.
4. The four capabilities are one connected engine (question → impact), never four cards.
5. Honest by construction — real structural facts stand in for social proof; nothing fabricated.
6. Mobile quality = desktop quality; motion clarifies and always respects reduced-motion.
